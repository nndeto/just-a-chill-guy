STORY
You are a hero on a quest. For what? You don't quite remember. Get started to see if you can figure it out. 

QUEST
To arrive at a castle for a birthday party all the hero's friends are throwing him. Racoon, Anomalocaris, and Cat. 
Happy Birthday message and raining confetti happen at this point.
