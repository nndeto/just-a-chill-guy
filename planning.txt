STORY
You are a hero on a quest. For what? You don't quite remember. Get started to see if you can figure it out. 

QUEST
To arrive at a castle for a birthday party all the hero's friends are throwing him. Raccoon, Anomalocaris, and Cat. 

STATS - Randomize and display
--> Swimming 
    1 - 10
--> Jumping
    1 - 10
--> luck
    1 - 10
--> Points
    Start at 5


LOCATIONS
- PATH
    --> You start before a split path
        --> If you go left, you go to the RIVER
        --> If you go right, you got to the FLOWER FIELD
- RIVER
    --> Swim across
            --> If swimming is 7-10, you make it across 
            --> If swimming is 4-6, you are swept away, but wash up on the opposite bank, and 1 point is subtracted
            --> If swimming is 1-3, you drown, and get GAME OVER
    --> Look for a bridge
            --> You find the bridge, cross successfully, and continue on the PATH
    --> Wait for a boat to pass by and take you across (50/50)
            --> No boat comes, you get hungry, go home, and get a GAME OVER as you never finished the quest
            --> A boat comes, but it takes awhile, and 2 points are subtracted
- FLOWER FIELD
    --> Walk past
            --> You contine on the PATH in too much of a hurry, and lose 2 points
    --> Stop and admire flowers 
            --> You gain 1 point for enjoying the little things
    --> Pick flowers
            --> A bee flies out a flower, stings you, and get GAME OVER (33% likely)
            --> You gain a bouquet of flowers, gain 2 points, and continue on the PATH (INVENTORY ADD) (66% likely)
- CASTLE (arrive after either RIVER or FLOWER FIELD)
    --> Try to open the door 
            --> If luck is 6-10, the door opens, and get INSIDE CASTLE
            --> If luck is 1-5, you receive a message to search for a key or another way in, lose 1 point
                --> Takes you to LOOK AROUND 
    --> Look around
            --> You come to a WINDOW with a box beneath it 
                --> If jumping is 7-10, you climb on the box, make it to the window, and get INSIDE CASTLE
                --> If jumping is 4-6, you have to jump again, lose 2 points, the get INSIDE CASTLE
                --> if jumping is 1-3, you fall, hit your head, and get a GAME OVER
            --> You get distracted by a tree
                --> If luck is 5-10, you think to check the hollow, and find a key (INVENTORY ADD)
                    --> You return to the DOOR and open it (KEY DISAPPEARS AS IS USED), get INSIDE CASTLE
                --> If luck is 1-4, a squirrel runs by and startles you, you lose 1 point, you continue looking around
                    --> find the WINDOW
- INSIDE CASTLE
    --> Go upstairs
        --> Feel eerie at the empty rooms, feel like you hear creepy whispering, decide to go home scared, get a GAME OVER
    --> Go downstairs
        --> Find your friends who jump out and surprise you
        --> If you have a flower bouquet in INVENTORY, they put it in a vase in the middle of the table 
        --> get the HAPPY BIRTHDAY ending
            --> Happy Birthday message and raining confetti happen
    --> Go to the kitchen
        --> Find a mess (powdered sugar, candles, spilled juice) that implies the castle is not empty, keep searching
            --> Go UPSTAIRS
            --> GO DOWNSTAIRS
- SECRET ENDING
    --> If points are above =>4, you get a bonus SECRET ENDING(tbd), after the HAPPY BIRTHDAY ending
        --> 1 redeemable date with me coupon


MECHANICS
- GAME START
    --> Roll stats
    --> Clear INVENTORY
    --> Reset POINTS to 5
- Game is one way forward, cannot replay scenes or choices without restarting
- All stats are generated w/Math.random at the beginning
- GAME OVER
    --> Option to PLAY AGAIN
